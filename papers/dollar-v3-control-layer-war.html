Dollar v3 / The Control Layer War

Medium · Dec 2025 to Jan 2026 · Policy / Payments / Stablecoins

Description current as of March 2026.

How the control layer and token types together determine where risk and policy attach. Maps how payment stablecoins, tokenized deposits, and tokenized cash products diverge in crisis, and how screening, holds, routing defaults, disclosures, and recordkeeping become the effective control layer. Three dollar objects that look identical in a wallet but become different instruments in stress; GENIUS and CLARITY concentrate compliance liability at the control layer.
