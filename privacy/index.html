Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2026-05-18. This page describes how Tokenization Systems handles personal data collected via the email newsletter sign-up form and via the site itself.

Newsletter sign-up

When you submit your email address through the newsletter sign-up form on this site, the address is sent to our email service provider (Buttondown). Buttondown stores subscriber data on its infrastructure and handles list management, double opt-in confirmation, and unsubscribe processing. Buttondown's privacy policy is available at buttondown.email/privacy.

We use your email address only to send the Tokenization Systems research newsletter (approximately monthly cadence; new papers, framework updates, regulatory events, reading-list highlights). We do not sell, rent, or transfer subscriber addresses to third parties.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter issue. Unsubscription is processed immediately by Buttondown; the address is removed from the active list.

Site analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (gtag.js) to measure visitor traffic and page popularity. Google Analytics collects aggregate data about page views, referrers, and approximate location (country level). It does not collect personally identifiable information. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

The site implements Google Consent Mode v2. By default, advertising-related signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, personalization_storage) are set to denied because the site runs no ads and performs no remarketing. Analytics, functionality, and security storage are granted by default to support visitor measurement and basic site features. The site does not display a cookie banner; if you visit from a jurisdiction that requires explicit consent for analytics cookies, you may disable cookies in your browser settings to prevent analytics collection.

Contact for privacy questions

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of any data we hold, write to zach@tokenization.systems.
