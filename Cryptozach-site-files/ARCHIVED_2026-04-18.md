- B1 paper title is now `Adaptive Tokenomics: A Systems Engineering Approach to Programmable Incentive Design` (older drafts here may say `Tokenomics as Systems Engineering: ...` or reference `Capital, Labor, Land`).
- B2 is now `Governance Concentration Beyond Token Allocation: An Institutional Design Analysis of DePIN and DeFi` (older drafts say `Tokenomics as Institutional Design`).
- B3 is now `Who Burns the Tokens? Fiscal Sustainability and Demand Concentration in GeoDePIN Networks` (older drafts say `GeoDePIN: Design Patterns for ...`).
- The site framework now tracks four dollar objects on overview/frameworks (deposit tokens added since the Dollar v3 essay); the Dollar v3 paper itself remains at three.
- Tokenized Equity Vehicles is the canonical short name (older drafts may say `Tokenized Equity` or `Three Tokenization Strategies`).
- Program paper count is eight (older drafts may say seven).

For the current canonical naming and inventory, see `docs/EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md` and `docs/SITE_STATE.md`.

## Recovery

If you need a snapshot of the older draft framing for any reason (provenance, citation history, "what did we say in February?"), the files in this directory are the source. Otherwise, prefer the live site or `docs/`.

## Removal policy

Do not delete this directory until at least one full audit cycle has confirmed that nothing in `docs/`, `papers/`, `research/`, `resume/`, or any other live-site path depends on these archived drafts.
