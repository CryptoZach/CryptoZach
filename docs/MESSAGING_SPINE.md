# Site messaging spine (editorial lock)

Repeat across entry pages when each may be a first visit. Do not repeat other boilerplate that only restates headings or page structure.

## Three lines

1. **Thesis:** Who routes the dollar matters more than which dollar is routed.
2. **Operator line:** Classify the token, but regulate and audit the operator.
3. **Proof stack:** Seven papers, flagship under FRBNY review, evidence-led research across two tracks.

## Vocabulary

- **Gateway** = the routing entity (issuer, exchange, protocol that processes stablecoin flows).
- **Control layer** = the analytical frame (infrastructure where compliance, routing, and economics concentrate).
- **Operator** = the accountable actor (entity with regulatory obligations and operational liability).

Do not rotate among gateway, operator, interface, control surface, or control points unless that distinction is doing analytical work on that specific page.

## Editing rule

A heading already does setup. The first sentence under it must add **proof**, **differentiation**, or **action**. If it restates the heading, explains why the section exists, or describes the structure of the work, cut it.
