About Tokenization Systems

Tokenization Systems is an independent research, advisory, and infrastructure practice focused on digital financial systems. It produces scholarly work on stablecoins, tokenized deposits, settlement infrastructure, and token-economy design. The program serves five audiences: Federal Reserve and policy designers, institutional asset managers, stablecoin and payments strategy teams, AI governance and infrastructure programs, and risk and controls leaders.

The research holds itself to the standard it applies to the systems it studies: claims trace to sources, frameworks are tested against adversarial conditions, and boundaries are named explicitly. Every claim is replicable. The work is US-focused and independent.

Researcher Zach Zukowski. Six years as Senior Investment Analyst and founding research hire at Borderless Capital, a Web3-focused registered investment adviser.

Frameworks Control Layer Intensity Index (CLII) for gateway regulatory scoring. Minimum Viable Equivalence Pack (MVEP) for institutional tokenization diligence.

Program status (April 20, 2026) Eight working papers on SSRN and eight federal comment letters filed across the GENIUS Act and related dockets, spanning seven rulemakings, six docket IDs, to five federal agencies, on a 10-day submission window. Both the SSRN and federal-comment layers of the current program are complete to date.

Papers All eight program papers in Tracks A and B are available on SSRN, with the accompanying Track A briefing pages (Dollar v3, Control Layer War, Tokenized Equity) published on the site.
