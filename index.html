About

About the author.

Zach Zukowski is a Senior Investment Analyst and founding research hire at Borderless Capital, a Web3-focused registered investment adviser. His independent research program operates under the Tokenization.Systems brand, producing scholarly work for Fed and policy audiences, institutional asset managers, and stablecoin and payments strategy teams.

The program holds itself to the standard it applies to the systems it studies: claims trace to sources, frameworks are tested against adversarial conditions, and boundaries are named explicitly. The work is US-focused, independent, non-advocacy, and every claim is replicable.
