### How I Help Teams Make Decisions (3-block)

**Block 1: Explain the system in plain English:** I translate policy, market structure, and technical design into questions non-specialists can follow: who issues the product, where the reserves sit, how money moves, who can pause or route flows, and what changes under stress.

**Block 2: Test whether a tokenized product is ready for institutional use:** I focus on custody, redemption, settlement, whether a payment is truly final, concentration, disclosures, and whether the tokenized version really works like the traditional asset.

**Block 3: Turn research into action:** I build decision frameworks: if-then scenario maps, evidence checklists, monitoring thresholds, and clear decision memos. The goal is simple: move forward, ask for more proof, redesign the product, or walk away.

**Intro:** A few starting points for readers who want clear, substantive answers in relatable language. The broader agenda spans stablecoin infrastructure, tokenized-product diligence, and token-economic simulation and modeling.

## Focus Page

**Kicker:** Focus
**H1:** How the research is structured
**Subhead:** A mid-depth overview of the operating model, core frameworks, measurement system, and current agenda behind the work.

### Background

Ten years in digital assets. Founding hire at Borderless Capital. Built the research function, led a four-analyst team, and supported diligence across about 250 investments, about $500M in assets under management, and 100+ tokenization engagements.

### Unifying Thesis

Regulate and audit the operator and the router, not the token. The decisive risks usually sit in the gateway, custody, routing, reserve, and redemption layers that turn policy into operating reality.

### Core Frameworks

**CLII:** A scoring system for custody segregation, reconciliation quality, and concentration risk across gateway models.

**MVEP:** A nine-gate diligence pack for testing whether the tokenized version really works like the traditional asset under stress.

**Credit migration model:** A five-lever framework for tracking how balances and credit move across bank and nonbank balance sheets as policy changes.

### How the Research Is Run

- Questions ranked by the cost of being wrong.
- Evidence tied to named data, versioned sources, and repeatable checks.
- Metrics chosen because they change decisions, owners, or escalation paths.
- Publication gated on readiness, with clear records of what would reopen a question.

### Current Focus Areas

**Private money and credit migration:** How stablecoins, tokenized deposits, and cash-management products reroute liabilities, credit creation, and bank funding.

**Settlement and market infrastructure:** How finality, official record, liquidity timing, and cross-system dependencies behave under scale and stress.

**The firms and systems that control access, routing, and holds:** Where policy becomes operations: interfaces, holds, reporting duties, custody design, and routing defaults.

**Governance and stress dynamics:** How infrastructure failure, concentration, and feedback systems shape outcomes in token networks and market plumbing.
