## Supporting research (four cards)

### A1 — Routing the Dollar

- **Status badge:** Under review
- **Link:** `/papers/routing-the-dollar` (SSRN 6267698)
- **What it is:** A data-driven study of 19 gateway entities and 51 wallet addresses showing that the same dollar token inherits different regulatory obligations, different Fed-policy sensitivity, and different crisis behavior depending on who routes it. Introduces the CLII, which measures how heavily regulated each gateway is across five dimensions.
- **Why it matters:** Moves analysis from token labels to operators, reserve chains, and routing networks, with named metrics for concentration and stress behavior.
- **Who it's for:** Policy, central bank, market-structure, and infrastructure readers building transferable analogies to AI routing and operator accountability.

### A2 — Minimum Viable Equivalence Pack (MVEP)

- **Status badge:** Public
- **Link:** `/papers/mvep` (SSRN 6363138)
- **What it is:** Ten diligence categories, each with a required artifact and pass or fail test, for whether a tokenized product maintains equivalence under stress.
- **Why it matters:** Turns diligence from pitch-deck review into a repeatable committee standard with explicit evidence requirements.
- **Who it's for:** Risk committees, tokenization platforms, custodians, legal and compliance teams, and policy designers who need the same rigor for AI product reviews.

### B2 — Governance Concentration Beyond Token Allocation

- **Status badge:** Under review
- **Link:** No standalone paper page yet; research index entry at `/research#institutional-design` (writing sample via `/contact`)
- **What it is:** Turns five theories of institutional fairness and accountability into testable claims evaluated against governance data from 12 live protocols, with governance concentration as the main evidence test.
- **Why it matters:** Gives the program a criteria layer for evaluating protocol governance alongside simulation results, connecting token and infrastructure design to standards of institutional legitimacy.
- **Who it's for:** Governance designers, DAO researchers, protocol teams, and AI governance readers who need explicit fairness and accountability criteria.

### A4 — Three Tokenization Strategies

- **Status badge:** Public
- **Link:** `/papers/tokenized-equity`
- **What it is:** Settlement-architecture analysis of how three institutional tokenization strategies share transfer-agent and market-infrastructure dependencies, including how a single transfer-agent stress window can degrade multiple strategies at once.
- **Why it matters:** Shows how settlement, custody, and ledger infrastructure choices concentrate operational responsibility and failure modes across products that look different on the surface.
- **Who it's for:** Asset managers, market-infrastructure leads, and product strategists who need a shared vocabulary with policy and risk teams, including AI deployment teams that mirror custody and settlement questions.
