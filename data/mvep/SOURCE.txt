MVEP scorecard data mirrored on tokenization.systems

mvep_scorecard_data.csv         Exhibit 1 of the MVEP paper: the consolidated MVEP v4 scorecard,
                                eleven rows (the ten categories plus the recursive Cash Settlement
                                Asset companion test) by fourteen products, 154 cells, each a verdict
                                with a source tag pointing to where the paper establishes it.
mvep_composite_rating_data.csv  Exhibit 3: the per-product composite rating record, computed from
                                Exhibit 1 by the floor (worst-verdict) rule per dimension plus a
                                1.0 to 5.0 strength score.

Upstream: https://github.com/CryptoZach/mvep-replication (exhibits/exhibit_01_mvep_scorecard and
exhibits/exhibit_03_mvep_composite_rating), the replication package of "Minimum Viable Equivalence
Pack: An Institutional Diligence Framework for Tokenized Products", SSRN abstract 6363138.
Mirrored 2026-10-12 from the main branch, byte for byte. The verdict vocabulary, the source tags and
the rating rule are documented in each exhibit's CAPTION.md upstream and in the paper's
Supplementary Materials. These two files drive the interactive scorecard on /frameworks/
(scripts/render_mvep_scorecard.py stamps them into the page; --check in CI).
