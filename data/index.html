Data and code

Data and code.

What can be downloaded, reproduced and cited: the datasets the site maintains and the replication material behind each paper. Research content is published under CC BY 4.0; code carries the licence stated in its repository.

Datasets the site maintains

These files are regenerated from one registry whenever a letter is filed or an outcome is verified, so they are as current as the pages that cite them. The registry's last verification date is inside each file.

DatasetFormatContents
Letters registryJSONEvery federal comment letter and the international response: docket, comment period, agencies, frameworks, recommendations with their status, the proceedings' outcomes and the watch list.
Recommendation indexCSVOne row per recommendation: letter number, agency, filing date, proceeding, the ask, the full text, topics, status and a link to the anchor on the letter page.
GENIUS Act rulemaking trackerJSONOne record per implementing rulemaking: identifiers, Federal Register publication, comment close, status, sources, next dates and the statutory frame.

Provenance in SOURCE.txt.

The letters, compiledPDFAll federal letters in filing order with a contents page and bookmarks.

Codebook for the letters registry

  • letters[]: slug, number (null for the international response), series (federal or international), kind, title, headline, filed (ISO date), agency (filter key) and agencies (the parties), proceeding (key into proceedings), docket (label, URL, RIN, Federal Register citation, comment tracking number, filing channel), comment_period (open, close), cycle, frameworks, papers, pdf_url, thesis, rec_count and recommendations[].
  • recommendations[]: n, id (the anchor on the letter page), ask (a short form), text, topics, status (one of recommendation_statuses) and status_note.
  • proceedings{}: name, agency, identifier, Federal Register citation, kind, URL and outcome (state from outcome_states, status label, note, sources, verification date, final action when one exists).
  • watch[]: dated next steps with sources. The CSV carries the same recommendation fields flattened, one row per recommendation.
