Statutory frame

  • July 18, 2025: Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act (GENIUS Act), S. 1582 signed into law.
  • July 18, 2026: statutory rulemaking deadline. Section 13 directs the primary federal payment stablecoin regulators to issue implementing rules within one year of enactment.
  • Effective date: The Act takes effect on the earlier of January 18, 2027 (18 months after enactment) or 120 days after the primary federal payment stablecoin regulators issue final implementing regulations.
  • July 18, 2028: From three years after enactment, digital asset service providers may offer or sell only payment stablecoins issued by permitted payment stablecoin issuers to persons in the United States.
  • Text: GENIUS Act, S. 1582 (119th Congress), enrolled text

How this tracker is kept

  • Rows come from one registry file, the same one that drives the letters page and its outcome ledger. A status changes here when a final rule, an interim final rule or a withdrawal is verified against the Federal Register, regulations.gov or the agency's own release, and the row keeps the source it was verified against.
  • The comment deadline shown is the one in the Federal Register notice. Where a notice gives only a formula (sixty days after publication), the row says so.
  • Rulemakings outside the program's own letters are included once identified; the tracker does not claim to be exhaustive on the day a new proposal appears. Corrections and additions: Zach@Tokenization.Systems.
